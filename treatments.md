## Frequently Asked Questions About BOTOX® and other neurotoxins such as Xeomin, Dysport, and Jeuveau.

What is BOTOX® & how does it work?

BOTOX® (onabotulinumtoxinA) is a neuromodulator that temporarily relaxes targeted facial muscles, reducing the appearance of dynamic wrinkles (i.e., lines that form with movements). By blocking the nerve signals that cause muscle contraction, BOTOX® softens frown lines, crow’s feet, forehead lines, and other expression wrinkles. Over time, repeated treatment can help retrain muscles to be less prone to causing deep crease formation.

Is BOTOX® Safe?

When administered by a licensed, experienced professional, BOTOX® is considered a very safe cosmetic treatment. Side effects are usually mild and temporary (bruising, swelling, slight asymmetry). We review your medical history to ensure safety.

Will BOTOX® make me look “frozen”?

No—at La Belle Vie Medical Care & Aesthetics, our approach emphasizes natural expression. The goal is to soften lines while preserving movement. You can still smile, frown, and emote—just with fewer wrinkles.

When will I see results, and how long do they last?

You may notice improvement in as little as 2–3 days, with full results typically achieved within ~14 days. Most patients enjoy results for 3–4 months; in some cases, this can last up to 5–6 months with good maintenance.

Does BOTOX® hurt?

Most people describe slight pinpricks. We can use topical numbing or ice if desired to reduce discomfort.

Am I a good candidate?

Ideal candidates are healthy adults who want to reduce dynamic facial lines. Those who are not eligible are pregnant or nursing patients, those with neuromuscular disorders, or individuals on certain medications. (We will review your medications during your consultation.)

How often should I come back?

We typically recommend repeat treatments every 3 to 4 months. With consistent treatments, some patients see longer-lasting responses.

Can I combine BOTOX® with other treatments?

Yes—combining microneedling, laser resurfacing, or skin care regimens with fillers often provides a more comprehensive rejuvenation. Don’t worry, we’ll tailor a plan to achieve your best results.

How much does BOTOX® cost?

The cost of BOTOX® varies depending on the dumber of units needed and the areas being treated. On average, BOTOX® in Utah ranges between $10 and $15 per unit, with most patients requiring 20 to 40 units per session to achieve their desired results. During your consultation, experienced injectors will assess your goals and create a personalized plan tailored to both your needs and budget.

Are there any risks to BOTOX®?

The Precautions and Risks of all Neurotoxins:

BOTOX®, Xeomin, Jeuveau, or Dysport injections may be ineffective against deeper wrinkles caused by excessive sun exposure; your La Belle Vie medical professional will advise you of any possible ineffective results during your consultation. Toxin treatment is not advisable for those who are pregnant or breastfeeding. Patients under a medication regimen that may interfere with the action of toxin treatments should consult with their primary care physician before seeking treatment.

## PDO Thread Lift Frequently Asked Questions:

What happens during the PDO Thread Treatment?

During a PDO Thread Lift, fine threads are gently inserted beneath the skin of the face or neck. Once placed, these threads trigger a controlled healing response known as a “selective inflammatory reaction,” which stimulates collagen production around the treated areas. Collagen—the body’s natural rejuvenator—helps improve skin firmness, elasticity, and overall youthfulness.

Similar to treatments like microneedling, fractionated laser resurfacing, or Profound RF, PDO Threads work by encouraging the skin to repair itself naturally. As threads lift and support lax skin, your body continues building new collagen along their path, leading to a longer-lasting, naturally refreshed appearance.

Is there downtime? What is the recovery like?

Recovery from a PDO Thread Lift is generally quick. Most patients experience mild swelling, tenderness, or slight bruising, which typically resolves within a week. Post-treatment discomfort is usually minimal and short-lived. In rare cases where a thread becomes visible or shifts, it can be easily adjusted or removed, and new threads may be placed. Overall, downtime is significantly shorter than a surgical facelift or neck lift.

Who is a good candidate for a PDO Thread Lift?

Ideal candidates are individuals who want to rejuvenate and lift the face or neck without undergoing surgery or general anesthesia. A PDO Thread Lift is an excellent option for those seeking subtle, natural improvements with minimal downtime and lower cost compared to traditional surgical procedures. Thread lifts can also complement other aesthetic treatments, such as Botox, dermal fillers, microneedling, or skin-resurfacing lasers, to enhance results.

What Areas Can Be Treated With PDO Threads?

PDO Threads are highly versatile and can be used on most areas of the face and body. The most common treatment areas include the cheeks, jawline, jowls, upper neck, and brows. They can also be used to tighten sagging skin on the upper arms, abdomen, thighs, and even around the knees—anywhere mild to moderate skin laxity is present.

## What to Expect for Your Sclerotherapy Procedure

Do not apply lotion to your legs before or after sclerotherapy. We recommend that you do not apply lotion to your legs, face or other treatment areas before or after your visit.

For Sclerotherapy, your professional aesthetician will use a fine needle to slowly inject your customized solution into the targeted vein. Compression and massage are then applied to disperse the solution. A compression pad may be placed on the treated area if you are having more than one sclerotherapy injection during your visit.

## Post-Treatment Care

After sclerotherapy, the treatment areas will be covered, and you will be required to wear compression socks. It is important that you stand up and walk around.

Moving your legs after the treatment will help prevent the formation of blood clots. Although you can resume normal activities, graduating compression stockings or bandages need to be worn for 1-3 weeks to maintain pressure on the treatment area(s).

## Common Side Effects

After sclerotherapy, you can generally return to normal activities within a day. As with any treatment, there may be common side effects, these may include:
- Bruising
- Redness
- Swelling
- Raised red areas
- Small skin sores
- Darkened Lines or Spots
- Multiple Tiny Bed Blood Vessels

Serious complications, although rare, should be addressed immediately, these may include:

## Frequently Asked Questions About CoolSculpting

How Does the CoolSculpting Treatment Work?

A CoolSculpting expert will start by marking the specific areas that require treatment and preparing the skin around those areas with a protective gel. Then the CoolSculpting wand is applied to the treatment areas for as little as 35 minutes. The patient may feel a little pressure from the wand suction and slight cold tingling sensation between minute three and five of the treatment process, but the area becomes numb afterward.

A deep massage is done immediately after the CoolSculpting treatment to mobilize the excess fat that has been released from the damaged fat cells. The massage will last for two or three minutes, and it will also help improve the lymphatic drainage of the fat. The actual treatment procedure is simple and straightforward. You can even read a book or bring a laptop during the treatment!

When Will I See Results from CoolSculpting?
People start noticing changes approximately three weeks after the CoolSculpting treatment, however, the most noticeable outcome will appear after two months. Your body will continue to flush out the excess fats several months after the treatment.
Is there Any Downtime?
Since the CoolSculpting procedure is non-surgical, you are free to resume your normal activities immediately after. Although some patients tend to experience minor bruising or redness of the treated areas this feeling goes away shortly after the procedure. You can return to work immediately.
Are There Any Side Effects of the Procedure?
There are no significant side effects associated with CoolSculpting apart from the redness and slight bruising of the treated areas, which is normal. Some patients may also experience numbness.
How Many Areas Can CoolSculpting Treat?

CoolSculpting is approved for treating stubborn fat in the abdomen, flanks, neck, bra fat, back fat, inner and outer thighs, upper arms, and underneath the buttocks. Treatments to different areas can be performed same day.

How Much Does CoolSculpting Cost?

The CoolSculpting cost will vary depending on the treatment areas and the number of CoolSculpting treatments needed. The larger areas tend to cost more compared to smaller areas. The procedure can be applied to eight body parts namely; underneath the buttocks, abdomen, inner and outer thighs, neck, flanks, back fat, bra fat and the upper arms.

How Many CoolSculpting Treatments Do I Need?

Everybody’s body differs, along with their goals in terms of health and physical appearance. Therefore, there is no one-size-fits-all treatment that is appropriate for each patient. Some people may only need one CoolSculpting session to achieve their goals, while others may require two to three sessions; especially if they are wanting to eliminate fat from a couple different areas. Your CoolSculpting specialist will create a treatment plan that is tailored to your needs and desired results.

Is CoolSculpting Permanent?

YES, CoolSculpting is permanent! The fat cells that are targeted by the controlled cooling process are killed during the treatment. In the following weeks, your body will naturally process and eliminate those now-dead cells, giving you long-term results that become most apparent one to three months.

Do You Lose Weight with CoolSculpting?

You could, but nothing major. Reducing fat and losing weight are different goals. CoolSculpting is aimed at helping you remove fat and sculpt your body, particularly from those common problem areas (abdomen, bra fat, neck, thighs, and upper arms). When people lose weight, their fat cells are simply reduced in size, but they remain. In comparison, CoolSculpting can reduce the actual number of fat cells in the treated area by about 20 to 25 percent. While you may notice no difference on the scale, you will look thinner and have a more sculpted body.

How Long Does a CoolSculpting Session Last?

CoolSculpting sessions are relatively short. The time of a session may vary based off the treatment plan you’ve devised with your CoolSculpting expert, but they generally will last between 35 and 60 minutes.

What Should You NOT Do Before CoolSculpting?

CoolSculpting is not an invasive procedure and is relatively easy on your body. Even still, there are few things you should avoid before going in for your procedure. First, make sure the skin in the area you are treating is healthy, devoid of cuts, bruises, sunburns, or other damage. You also should not tan the targeted area right before your session, as it can make the skin more sensitive. Second, do not take medications or supplements that thin your blood. This includes aspirin and other anticoagulants, as well as St John’s Wort, fish oils (omega 3 fatty acids), ginseng, ginger, ginkgo biloba, garlic, dong quai, ephedra, and feverfew. Drink plenty of water and eat nutritious food so your body is equipped to undergo the procedure.

Should You Massage After CoolSculpting?

Directly after the CoolSculpting treatment is completed, the CoolSculpting specialist will conduct a two- to three-minute deep-tissue massage to help improve the lymphatic drainage of the fat. You may also find massaging the area in the following weeks helps ease certain mild side effects, such as numbness or itching, by motivating blood flow.

Should I Wear Spanx After CoolSculpting?

Wearing Spanx after your CoolSculpting session will not increase the effectiveness or lead to better results, as the cells are killed during the treatment and your body will do the rest of the work in the following weeks. However, it is okay to wear compression.

Can CoolSculpting Cause Nerve Damage?

No, nerve damage is not a side effect of CoolSculpting. During the treatment, you may experience discomfort in the form of pinching, pulling, tingling, and stinging sensations that last a couple minutes before the area goes numb. Other temporary side effects at the site—such as redness, bruising, tenderness, cramping, aching, and sensitivity—will usually subside within a few days or weeks.

Who Should Not Have CoolSculpting Procedure?

CoolSculpting is not designed to be a weight-loss solution for those who are obese and require a more drastic solution, such as gastric bypass surgery. Additionally, individuals who suffer from paroxysmal cold hemoglobinuria, cryoglobulinemia, and cold agglutinin disease are not good candidates for CoolSculpting.

Does CoolSculpting Hurt?

No. Most people agree the CoolSculpting procedure is fairly painless, and no anesthesia is required. Some patients may experience a slight pressure from the wand suction, as well as a cold, tingling sensation, early on in the treatment. Within the first five minutes of the freezing applicator being placed, however, the treated area will become numb. In fact, the treatment causes so little discomfort, you can easily read a book, take a nap, watch television, work on your laptop, or do other activities during your session.

What is Renuva®?

Renuva® is an allograft adipose matrix. This means it is made from donated, purified fat tissue that has been carefully processed to remove all cellular components, leaving only the essential growth factors, proteins, and collagen that form the structural matrix of fat.

When Renuva® is injected into an area with volume loss, this matrix acts as a scaffold. It signals your body to send its own fat cells to the area. Over the next few months, your body gradually populates this framework with your own living fat cells, while the donated matrix is naturally absorbed. The result is new, natural tissue in the treated area. Book now to experience effectively restored volume where it has diminished.
