About Us

Aesthetic medicine rooted in integrity & care

Our Philosophy

Beauty that feels like you

At La Belle Vie, we believe that aesthetic medicine at its best is invisible — results that leave you looking refreshed, confident, and like the very best version of yourself. We don't believe in trends, overcorrection, or a one-size-fits-all approach.


Every client who walks through our doors is met with a genuine consultation, honest recommendations, and a treatment plan tailored specifically to their anatomy, goals, and lifestyle. We treat your health and beauty with the same care and discretion we would want for ourselves.

Natural Results

We enhance — never alter. Our treatments are designed to complement your features, not overwrite them.

Medical Excellence

Led by a Family Nurse Practitioner with 30+ years of clinical experience, safety and precision are non-negotiable.

Personalized Care

No templates, no shortcuts. Every plan is built around your individual concerns, timeline, and desired outcome.

Meet Our Founder

Kelly Lance

MSN, APRN, FNP-C

With over 30 years of experience as a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner, Kelly Lance founded La Belle Vie on a simple conviction: that every patient deserves individualized, medically sound care delivered in an environment that feels welcoming, private, and judgment-free.

Kelly brings a lifetime of clinical expertise to each treatment — from hormone replacement and complex injectables to advanced aesthetic procedures. Her approach balances the precision of a skilled medical provider with the sensibility of an artist, consistently delivering results that look natural and feel transformative.

Patients throughout Draper, Salt Lake, and surrounding Utah communities trust Kelly not only for her technical mastery, but for her genuine investment in their wellbeing. She takes the time to listen, educate, and ensure every client leaves feeling confident and cared for.
